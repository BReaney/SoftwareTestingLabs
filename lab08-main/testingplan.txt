This is the high level testing plan for the exploratory testing of ATM system 1.0, for Lab08 of SENG275, March 12th 2026

Approach: Test most functions a Little

TEST 1:
User: Operator
Function: System Startup
Action: Inputting a negative value for the amount of money in the ATM
Expected Result: Error message, cant have negative amount of 20 dollar bills
Actual Result:Error message, must input a value greater then or equal to zero

TEST 2:
User: Customer
Function: Session
Action: Inputting incorrect Card number
Expected Result: Error message
Actual Result: Passes, asked for pin, when pin inputted states invalid card and returns to insert card prompt

TEST 3:
User: Customer
Function: Session
Action: Inputting incorrect Pin number, then the correct Pin number
Expected Result: Successful access to the bank account
Actual Result: prompts user to reinput pin after incorrect number, but requires you to put it in twice as it does not recognize the first re attempt.

TEST 4:
User: Customer
Function: Session
Action: Inputting incorrect Pin number 3 times
Expected Result: Lockout of account, incorrect pin too many times
Actual Result: Lockout of account

TEST 5:
User: Customer
Function: Withdraw
Action: Attempting to withdraw more money then the ATM has
Expected Result: error message, cannot withdraw more money then what the ATM has on hand
Actual Result: error message, insufficient amount of money

TEST 6:
User: Customer
Function: Withdraw
Action: Attempting to withdraw more money then the account has
Expected Result: error message, cannot withdraw more money then what your bank account has (Sadly)
Actual Result: when attempting to withdraw 200 dollars out of an account with 100, withdraws 20. When attempting to withdraw 100 dollars out of the same account, proper error is given.

TEST 7:
User: Customer
Function: Deposit
Action: Attempt to deposit a negative amount of money
Expected Result: error message, cannot deposit a negative value
Actual Result: does not allow negative inputs, even better

TEST 8:
User: Customer
Function: Transfer
Action: Attempt to transfer more money then is in a account to another account
Expected Result: error message, transfering too much money
Actual Result: error message, transferring too much money

TEST 9:
User: Customer
Function: Transfer
Action: Transfer a negative value of money between two accounts
Expected Result: error message, cannot transfer negative money
Actual Result: does not allow negative inputs

TEST 10:
User: Customer
Function: Inquiry
Action: Attempt to make an inquiry to find out how much money is in your savings account
Expected Result: The correct amount of money in your savings account
Actual Result: the correct amount of money is displayed


TEST 11:
User: Operator
Function: System Startup
Action: Input a non integer into the amount of bills in the ATM
Expected Result: error message, cant put non integer amount of bills
Actual Result: error message, must put a value greater then or equal too 0
